Trip pilot

: a trip-planning pilot, run by for a few friends.

This is a small test, not a product. I'm asking a few people I know to plan one real trip each with a planner I've been building. It plans around values (trains before short flights, a carbon limit per day, local and independent places) and shows the tradeoffs with figures. It never books, pays or sends anything for you: you book on the seller's own page.

Before you sign in, please read this.

  1. No lawyer has reviewed this. I'm running the pilot without legal review, only with people I know. If that isn't OK with you, please don't sign up; there's no hard feeling.
  2. Where your data sits. In a database run by Supabase, a US company, in its US East (Northern Virginia) data centre, in a project I own. It is not run by Anthropic.
  3. Who can see it.
    • You see your own data. Other pilot travellers can't see yours unless you add them to a trip; then they see that trip.
    • Google handles sign-in, so it learns that you signed in to the pilot. It sees nothing you enter on the site.
    • I can see everything you enter, as the owner of the database. I'll look only to run the pilot and fix problems.
    • Planning runs in my own Claude account (Anthropic). When you share a trip and send me the file the site makes, that trip, your travel preferences, and each of your travellers' first name, nationality and passport expiry date go into my Claude chats, and Claude may read it through my account to plan, check and fix the pilot. Anthropic's handling of it follows my account's terms and settings. My Claude account is [plan]. Chats [are / aren't] used to train Anthropic's models.
  4. What it keeps: your Google account's email and account ID, and the name and profile-picture link Google sends when you sign in; a display name and home city; your travel preferences; the people you add as travellers (first and last name, nationality, passport expiry date only); your trips and what the planner writes for them; when you shared a trip with the planner; and this agreement.
  5. What it never keeps: passport, visa, Known Traveler or redress numbers; card or bank numbers; passwords or codes; a street address with an apartment or unit number. Please don't type them anywhere on the site. It tries to refuse them automatically, but that check can miss one. Please also keep health details out of notes.
  6. Backups. Supabase keeps daily backups for 7 days, so something you delete can stay in a backup for up to 7 days. [If the pilot is still on Supabase's free plan when you sign up: there are no backups, so a fault could lose data, and the site goes offline after about a week of little use until I restart it.] Keep your own copy of anything you need, especially your bookings.
  7. You're in control. Download your data (your sign-in record included), or delete it, at any time from the site. To close your sign-in account too, ask me. You can stop at any time.
  8. Other people. If you add someone travelling with you, you confirm they're OK with it.
  9. What I'll measure: from your trip, the share of travel by rail, nights in independent places and carbon per person per day; and I'll ask you at the end whether you'd take the trip. I'll keep these figures, without your name, in my planning notes in Claude. Apart from Supabase, Google's sign-in and the planner as above, I won't share your data with anyone.
  10. When it ends. At the end of the pilot I'll delete your data unless you ask me to keep it. Either way, I'll delete the Claude chats that hold the trips you shared.
