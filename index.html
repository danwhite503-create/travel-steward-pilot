The short version, with the exact wording one tap away.

  1. A small test with friends

    Full wording

    No lawyer has reviewed this. I'm running the pilot without legal review, only with people I know. If that isn't OK with you, please don't sign up; there's no hard feeling.

  2. Your data stays in one place

    A Supabase database in US East (Virginia), in a project I own.

    Full wording

    Where your data sits. In a database run by Supabase, a US company, in its US East (Northern Virginia) data center, in a project I own. It is not run by Anthropic.

  3. Who sees it

    You, anyone you add to a trip, and me (to run and fix the pilot). Google only learns you signed in. If you use the mic, your browser's speech service turns it into text; the pilot never gets the audio.

    Full wording

    You see your own data. Other pilot travelers can't see yours unless you add them to a trip; then they see that trip.

    Google handles sign-in, so it learns that you signed in to the pilot. It sees nothing you enter on the site.

    I can see everything you enter, as the owner of the database. I'll look only to run the pilot and fix problems.

    Other people. If you add someone traveling with you, you confirm they're OK with it.

    Speaking instead of typing. If you tap the mic, your browser's own speech service turns your voice into text: Google's in Chrome, Apple's in Safari (or your device itself, if it's set to). The pilot gets only the text, which you see before you send it; it never receives or keeps the audio. If you'd rather not, type, or use your phone keyboard's own mic.

  4. Claude plans with it

    The planner runs in my Claude account; Arlo, the live steward, runs on Anthropic's API. Neither trains on it; Anthropic says it deletes Arlo's messages within 30 days, longer only if flagged or the law requires. Arlo never books or sends anything.

    Full wording

    Planning runs in my own Claude account (Anthropic). When you share a trip with the planner, that trip, your travel preferences, and each of your travelers' first name, nationality and passport expiry date go into my Claude chats, and Claude may read it through my account to plan, check and fix the pilot. Anthropic's handling of it follows my account's terms and settings. My Claude account is [plan]. Chats [are / aren't] used to train Anthropic's models.

    Arlo, the live steward, runs on Anthropic's API. When you or someone on your trip writes to Arlo, that message, the trip's recent messages, its title, dates, brief and plan summary go to Anthropic's API through my server; when the trip's owner writes, also the owner's preferences and the first names and nationalities of the travelers going. Arlo works on a trip only when you and the owner have agreed to this version. Anthropic says it deletes these within 30 days (longer if its safety systems flag them, up to 2 years, or the law requires) and doesn't train on them without permission. Arlo can't book or send anything, and what he says about places, prices or times is a suggestion until checked.

  5. What's kept, and what never is

    Kept: your sign-in, preferences, travelers (name, nationality, passport expiry) and trips. Never: passport, visa or card numbers, passwords, or a unit number in an address.

    Full wording

    What it keeps: your Google account's email and account ID, and the name and profile-picture link Google sends when you sign in; a display name and home city; your travel preferences; the people you add as travelers (first and last name, nationality, passport expiry date only); your trips and what the planner writes for them; when you shared a trip with the planner; and this agreement.

    What it never keeps: passport, visa, Known Traveler or redress numbers; card or bank numbers; passwords or codes; a street address with an apartment or unit number. Please don't type them anywhere on the site. It tries to refuse them automatically, but that check can miss one. Please also keep health details out of notes.

  6. You're in control

    Download or delete your data any time. I'll measure rail share, independent nights and carbon per day, without your name, and delete everything when the pilot ends unless you ask me to keep it. There are no backups yet, so keep your own copy of your bookings.

    Full wording

    You're in control. Download your data (your sign-in record included), or delete it, at any time from the site. To close your sign-in account too, ask me. You can stop at any time.

    Backups. The pilot is on Supabase's free plan, so there are no backups: a fault could lose data, and the site goes offline after about a week of little use until I restart it. If I move to a paid plan, Supabase keeps daily backups for 7 days, so something you delete could then stay in a backup for up to 7 days; I'll update this notice first. Keep your own copy of anything you need, especially your bookings.

    What I'll measure: from your trip, the share of travel by rail, nights in independent places and carbon per person per day; and I'll ask you at the end whether you'd take the trip. I'll keep these figures, without your name, in my planning notes in Claude. Apart from Supabase, Google's sign-in and the planner as above, I won't share your data with anyone.

    When it ends. At the end of the pilot I'll delete your data unless you ask me to keep it. Either way, I'll delete the Claude chats that hold the trips you shared.
